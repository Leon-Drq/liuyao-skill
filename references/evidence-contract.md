# Evidence contract

Use four visibly separate layers in every substantive report.

## 1. Verified input

Record the exact question, time scope, source time zone, calendar convention, casting method, chart version, and any corrections. For images, record which images were usable and why. Never fill a missing required field from intuition.

## 2. Calculated or observed facts

List only machine-calculated chart fields or directly visible image/room features. Preserve raw names, positions, values, and source timestamps. A failed or partial calculation stays failed or partial.

## 3. Traditional interpretation

For each important claim, cite at least one fact from layer 2. Phrase it as a tendency, symbolic reading, or hypothesis. Use confidence labels only when useful:

- high: several independent chart or observation signals agree;
- medium: one strong signal or several mixed signals;
- low: incomplete input, school differences, weak image quality, or conflicting evidence.

Do not manufacture numerical probabilities.

## 4. Reality-based action

Translate the reading into one to three reversible actions, a condition to watch, and a review time. For a choice, include at least one non-mystical check such as cost, consent, contract terms, health symptoms, or direct communication.

## Follow-up record

Keep the original reading immutable. Append follow-ups with date, new evidence, what happened, and whether the earlier interpretation was supported, contradicted, or unresolved. Do not retroactively rewrite the original claim.
